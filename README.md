# GAIUS Ministry Reporting PWA v6

Registration now lists the six reporting department-leader roles individually:
1. Media Department Leader
2. Follow-up Department Leader
3. Service Coordination Department Leader
4. Counseling and Support Department Leader
5. Research and Training Department Leader
6. Event Department Leader

The selected leader role automatically assigns and locks the corresponding report department. Administrative and Finance Staff and Monday Staff Program Leader remain separate reporting roles. International Staff, Assisting Staff and Prayer Staff remain non-reporting roles.
